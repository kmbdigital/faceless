## Brand basics, as of 21 Sept 2026

**Positioning** — *I help midlife muslimahs make their first $1,000 online
without showing their face.*

**Fonts** — Libre Baskerville (headlines), Lato (body). Both Google Fonts.

**Colours**

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Navy — base | `#102A43` | Cooler, editorial. Book cover being restated to match. |
| Amber — accent | `#D6A23E` | Settled 21 Sept. Accent only. |
| Cream | `#FAF7F2` | Light backgrounds, body text on navy. |
| Charcoal | `#2B2B2B` | Body text on light. |

**The cue** — an amber rule under every headline: 6px tall, 96px wide fixed,
24px below the headline, flush left. Same every time. At thumbnail size it is
the thing doing the job a face would do.

**Contrast rules** — body text on navy is cream, never amber. Text sitting *on*
amber is charcoal, never white.

## Why amber

Pink `#E8628C` read too bright and too close to the old KMB brand. High chroma
is what reads young, so the fix was saturation rather than hue family. Rose and
clay were tried; gold vanished at 6px and signalled luxury, which alienates a
reader worried about money; marigold was legible but loud; teal made the whole
palette cool. Amber sits between deep gold and marigold.

Warm and inviting turned out to be carried by the cream ground, the
photography, the serif and the writing — not by the accent hue. The bar's only
job is to be seen and remembered.

**Photography** — own photographs, back view at a laptop, jeans and a navy
hijab. Same shoot, same framing. Not stock.
