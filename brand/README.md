## Brand basics, as of 21 Sept 2026

**Positioning** — *I help midlife muslimahs make their first $1,000 online
without showing their face.*

**Fonts** — Libre Baskerville (headlines), Lato (body). Both Google Fonts.

**Colours**

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Navy — base | `#102A43` | Cooler, editorial. Book cover being restated to match. |
| Pink — accent | `#E8628C` | **Under review** — reads too bright/young; looking for something warmer and more sophisticated. |
| Cream | `#FAF7F2` | Light backgrounds, body text on navy. |
| Charcoal | `#2B2B2B` | Body text on light. |

**The cue** — a pink rule under every headline: 6px tall, 96px wide fixed,
24px below the headline, flush left. Same every time. At thumbnail size it is
the thing doing the job a face would do.

**Photography** — own photographs, back view at a laptop, jeans and a navy
hijab. Same shoot, same framing. Not stock.
