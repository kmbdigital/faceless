Why light blue does not work as the cue, and it is not a taste judgement. An accent has to differ from the base in hue, not just brightness. Light blue is the same hue as the navy, so at thumbnail size the eye reads it as a faded piece of the background rather than a deliberate mark — the exact job the bar exists to do. It would work beautifully as a secondary colour inside a chart or a quote block. Just not as the thing that makes a pin recognisably yours.
