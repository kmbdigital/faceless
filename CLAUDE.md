# Faceless — channel brief and decisions of record

Source of truth for the faceless YouTube channel and its content pipeline.
Update this file when a decision changes; do not re-derive it from chat history.

## Audience

Women starting a new online business, specifically:

- Working a 9–5 and building on the side
- Women over 50 starting over, often with little or no retirement savings
- Muslimahs and other women for whom showing their face is not an option

## Why faceless

Faceless is a requirement of the audience, not a stylistic preference.

For muslimahs and other modesty-observant women, "just talk to camera" is not
available advice. A faceless channel demonstrates the thing they need proven:
that this is buildable without showing your face. Voice and story carry trust.

The niche on YouTube is dominated by women talking to camera, so reach is
harder to win. That cost is accepted — it buys credibility with the segment
nobody else is serving.

## Angle

Practitioner, not teacher. What is actually being built and automated to run a
business while working full time. Said in first person, from inside the work.

## Cadence

- **Publish:** 2 videos per month
- **Film:** batched, 2 videos per session — one filming session per month
- **Pipeline runs:** once per month, producing 2 complete video packages
- Batches may grow to 4 later; 2 is the committed starting point

## Funnel

Video → **Week One plan** (free, opinionated 5-day plan that makes the choices
for her) → email list → **the sprint**.

Nothing else gets built until this runs.

### The sprint

- Two weeks, fixed start date, 5–6 women
- One call a week, one 15-minute task a day, a public commitment
- Sold on the calendar and the room, not the information
- First-run price: $97–$297
- Engine: daily email or text with today's one task plus a paste-ready AI prompt

A planner was considered and rejected on 17 Sept — she already owns three, and
buying another is the research-mode behaviour the offer exists to break.
